# All About White Cats

White cats are widely loved for their striking appearance and unique physical traits. Whether pure white or featuring subtle markings, these feline companions have fascinated humans for centuries.

## Key Facts & Characteristics

* **Genetics:** A pure white coat is typically caused by the dominant white gene ($W$), which masks all other color patterns and traits.
* **Eye Color:** White cats often have striking eye colors, including blue, green, yellow, or copper. Heterochromia (having two different colored eyes, such as one blue and one green) is also common among white cats.
* **Deafness:** There is a known genetic link between the dominant white coat gene, blue eyes, and congenital deafness. However, not all white cats are deaf!
* **Sun Sensitivity:** Because white cats have less pigment in their skin and ears, they are more susceptible to sunburn and require extra protection from direct, intense sunlight.
